# VisitPaper privacy policy

Effective date: September 26, 2026

Developer: Vali Mikayilov

## Information in the app

VisitPaper stores the property details, client names, business names, visit dates, checklist entries, notes, and selected photo copies you enter on your device. It does not operate a developer-hosted server, require an account, or upload this content to the developer.

The app contains no advertising SDKs, third-party analytics, tracking, in-app purchases, or subscriptions. The developer does not collect app usage or location data through the app.

## Photos

Apple's photo picker lets you select specific photos. VisitPaper imports copies of your selected photos for use in reports. The imported copies are resized and re-encoded without copying source GPS or EXIF metadata. Original library photos are not modified. Apple's Photos or iCloud services may download selected images according to your Apple settings.

## Sharing and backups

You choose whether to export or share a report. Reports can include the names, addresses, notes, dates, and photos you supplied. The selected destination receives the information you share and handles it under its own terms and privacy practices.

Device backups, system diagnostics, and Apple's store services are controlled by Apple and your system settings. This policy describes VisitPaper itself; it does not change Apple's policies or backup behavior.

## Retention and deletion

Saved visits remain on your device until you delete them or remove app data. When a saved visit is deleted, photo copies that are no longer referenced by saved visits are removed from the app's photo storage. Discarded draft photo copies may remain until the next successful save or deletion performs storage cleanup. Temporary report exports are stored in the operating system's temporary directory; copies shared elsewhere are outside the app's control.

Deleting an app record does not remove original library photos or copies you have shared with other people or services.

## Support

If you submit a GitHub support issue, GitHub hosts that communication under its privacy policy and the developer can read and respond to it. Support issues are public. Do not include private customer records or photos.

[Contact the developer through the support issue tracker](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues/new).

## Changes

Changes to the app's data practices will be reflected in an updated policy with a revised effective date.
