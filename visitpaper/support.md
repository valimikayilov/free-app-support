# VisitPaper support

VisitPaper organizes a cleaning visit into a room checklist and exports a PDF for your client. All features are free. No account, advertisements, or subscription is required.

## Create a visit

Choose **New visit**, enter a property name, and add the details you want included in the report. The property name is required. Address, client, and business fields are optional.

Open an area to edit its name, check its completion status, add before-and-after photos, and enter notes. Use **Save** to save the visit. Cancelling with unsaved changes asks whether to discard them.

Photos are selected through Apple's system photo picker. VisitPaper copies selected images into its own storage, resizes them for reports, and does not modify the originals. Cloud-only photos may need an internet connection to download through Apple's photo picker before importing. Afterwards the visit can be edited without an internet connection.

## Share a report

Choose **Preview & share PDF**. This saves your changes and opens the report. Choose **Share PDF** to save or share a copy using available system sharing options. VisitPaper does not send reports automatically.

Unchecked areas and missing photos are identified in the report. The visit date is entered by you and does not certify when a photo was captured.

## Start the next visit

Choose **Start next visit**. The app saves the current visit, then prepares a new one with the same property details and area names. The new visit has a new date and no completed checks, notes, or photos. Save the new visit when ready.

## Storage and deletion

Visits are stored on this device. There is no VisitPaper cloud account or automatic synchronization. Export reports you want to keep. A PDF export is a readable report, not an editable backup of the app's records. Removing the app or its storage can remove saved records.

Delete a visit from its list menu. The app asks for confirmation. This deletes the saved visit and photo copies no longer used by other saved visits. It does not delete originals in Photos or report copies you previously shared.

[Open a support issue](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues/new) with your app version, operating system version, and steps to reproduce the issue. GitHub issues are public; omit private customer information. A GitHub account is needed to submit an issue.
