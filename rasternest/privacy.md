# RasterNest privacy policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026

RasterNest does not collect data. It has no developer server, account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, in-app purchases, or third-party SDKs.

## Local processing and storage

Images you select are read and processed on your Mac. RasterNest uses macOS permission dialogs to access selected files and export folders. Exported images stay in the destination you choose. The app does not upload your images, filenames, metadata, or export history to the developer.

Current settings and saved recipes are stored locally in the app's container. The batch list and source-file permissions are not restored after quitting. Sample images are generated inside the app's local temporary storage. Uninstalling the app or removing its container may remove saved recipes; exported files are stored separately.

Exports omit source photo metadata such as EXIF and GPS. This does not remove personal information visible in image pixels or filenames. The operating system may add ordinary file-system information to saved files.

## External links

Support and privacy links open GitHub in your browser. GitHub's own privacy practices apply when you visit that service. GitHub support issues are public and require a GitHub account. Do not post private images or personal information.

## Contact and changes

For questions, [open a support issue](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues/new). This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
