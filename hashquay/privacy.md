# HashQuay privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

HashQuay does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertisements, tracking, analytics, third-party SDKs, or network entitlement.

The app reads files you select or drop, calculates checksums on your Mac, and keeps file references and results in memory. It reads a manifest and the containing folder only when you choose them. Original files are not modified. Reports and manifests are written only to destinations you choose and contain filenames, byte counts, and checksums. Full source paths are not included in these exports. You control whether to share exported information.

Copy writes the selected checksum to the macOS clipboard. Paste reads text from the clipboard only when you press the Paste button or use a standard paste action. The app does not monitor the clipboard. Included demonstration files are written to its own temporary directory.

The Help and Privacy Policy commands open your browser to this public support repository. GitHub and your browser have their own privacy practices. If you voluntarily open a support issue, its contents are public; please avoid personal or confidential information.

Contact: [public support issues](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues).
