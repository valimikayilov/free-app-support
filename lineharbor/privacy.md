# LineHarbor privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

LineHarbor does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertisements, tracking, analytics, third-party SDKs, or network entitlement.

Text you type, paste, or import is processed locally on your Mac and retained in memory for the current workspace. Imported source files are not modified. Drafts and HTML reports are saved only when you request them, to locations you choose. These exports include both complete texts and their titles; drafts also include comparison options. You control whether to share these files. The app does not autosave your workspace.

Paste reads the macOS clipboard only when you press a Paste button or use a standard paste action. Standard text copy writes the selected text to the clipboard. The app does not monitor the clipboard.

The Help and Privacy Policy commands open your browser to this public support repository. GitHub and your browser have their own privacy practices. If you voluntarily open a support issue, its contents are public; avoid personal or confidential information.

Contact: [public support issues](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues).
