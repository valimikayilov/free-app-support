# MeridianPane privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

MeridianPane does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. It has no accounts, advertisements, tracking, analytics, third-party SDKs, or network entitlement.

Your chosen time zones, location labels, working hours, clock preference, and meeting title, notes, duration, and last valid time are processed locally. They are automatically saved in a JSON file in the app’s sandboxed local Application Support folder. Importing a setup creates a local backup of the previous setup. Resetting an unreadable setup first copies the original file to a backup. Backups remain local until you manage them yourself through Show local storage.

Setup exports contain your locations, hours, preferences, and meeting details. Calendar exports and copied summaries include the meeting title, notes, and times. Files are saved only to locations you choose, and you control whether to share them or import them into a calendar. MeridianPane does not access your calendars, contacts, or precise location, and does not send invitations.

Copy summary writes to the macOS clipboard only when requested. Normal text editing supports standard copy and paste. The app does not monitor the clipboard.

Help and Privacy Policy open this public repository in your browser. GitHub and your browser have their own privacy practices. Support issues are public; avoid personal or confidential information.

Contact: [public support issues](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues).
