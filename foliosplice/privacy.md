# FolioSplice privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

FolioSplice is developed by Vali Mikayilov. The app does not collect or transmit user data to the developer or third-party services. It contains no analytics, advertising, accounts, purchases, or third-party SDKs and has no network entitlement.

## PDF files and workspace

PDFs you select are read locally for page previews and assembly. The workspace, undo history, and loaded source pages remain in memory until you clear the workspace or quit. FolioSplice does not automatically save your PDFs or workspace. Export writes files only to the location you choose. Original files are not modified, and existing files are not overwritten. The operating system may separately maintain ordinary app/window state or diagnostics under its own settings.

## External links and support

Support and privacy links open in your browser. GitHub and your browser operate under their own privacy policies. Information you voluntarily include in a support issue is handled by GitHub and may be public. Please do not post confidential PDFs or sensitive personal information.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change. Privacy questions can be raised in the [support repository](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues) without sharing sensitive information.
