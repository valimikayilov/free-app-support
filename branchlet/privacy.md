# Branchlet privacy policy

Effective September 26, 2026.

Branchlet does not collect data. It has no developer-operated server, advertising, analytics, tracking, account system, or third-party SDKs.

## On-device storage

Branchlet saves puzzle layouts, tile positions, undo history, move and hint counts, daily-puzzle dates, the selected game mode, and completion counts in its local app storage. This information is not sent to the developer. Operating-system backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Removing the app or its storage can remove saved progress. There is no Branchlet cloud account or automatic cross-device synchronization.

## Sharing and external links

Sharing a puzzle code uses the operating system's sharing interface. The code contains a board size and numeric seed; it does not contain identity or progress information. You choose the destination and complete the sharing action. That destination's own privacy policy applies.

Support and privacy links open GitHub. Visiting GitHub is subject to GitHub's privacy policy. Support issues are public and require a GitHub account to submit. Please omit private information.

Apple may separately process App Store transactions, downloads, diagnostics, or backups under its policies and your device settings. Branchlet contains no custom diagnostic collection.

## Contact

For questions, [open a support issue](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues/new). This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
