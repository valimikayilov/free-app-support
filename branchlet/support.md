# Branchlet support

Branchlet is a free connection puzzle. It has no account, ads, purchases, or subscriptions.

## Connect the branches

Tap or click a tile to rotate it clockwise. The gold starting tile stays fixed. Connect every tile back to that starting tile, with no branch pointing off the board or into a missing connection.

Connected tiles have purple branches and filled centers. Unconnected tiles have hollow centers. On a keyboard, use Tab to focus a tile, arrow keys to move, and Space to rotate.

**Undo** restores an earlier turn. **Hint** sets one tile to its orientation in a known solution. A hint remains counted if you undo it. The game accepts any valid completed network, not only the generated solution. There is no timer or move limit.

## Practice and daily puzzles

Choose a new 4 × 4, 6 × 6, or 8 × 8 practice puzzle. Starting another practice puzzle replaces the saved practice board. The latest daily board is saved separately.

The daily 6 × 6 puzzle uses the UTC date on your device, so its date can differ from your local date near midnight. Opening Daily on a new UTC day replaces the previous daily board. Playing does not require internet access.

**Share puzzle code** opens the system sharing options. You choose whether and where to share. Another player can use **Play a code** to recreate the board. The code contains a size and seed; it does not include your moves, progress, or identity. Entering a code replaces your practice puzzle.

## Saved progress

Progress saves on this device after each change. There is no automatic synchronization between devices. Operating-system backups may include saved progress. Removing the app or its storage can remove that progress.

If saving fails, the app displays an error and a retry button. Keep the app open and retry after resolving the storage problem. If a saved file cannot be read, Branchlet preserves it and stops changes to avoid overwriting it.
