# PulseCove privacy policy

Effective September 27, 2026.

PulseCove does not collect, transmit, sell, or track personal data. It has no accounts, advertising, analytics, third-party SDKs, backend, or network entitlement. No microphone or recording permission is requested. Clicks are synthesized locally from your project settings.

The current project and recovery copies stay in the app's sandbox on your Mac. JSON imports are read only when you choose them. WAV audio and JSON exports are written only to the location you select. Those locations may be synchronized by another service you use, such as iCloud Drive; that is outside PulseCove's control.

Help and privacy links open GitHub pages in your browser. GitHub's own privacy policy applies there. Support issues are public, so do not include private files or personal information.

Deleting PulseCove may leave exported files or backups you chose to keep. You control those files in Finder. Contact the developer through the [support repository](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues).
