# RecallPier privacy policy

Last updated September 27, 2026.

RecallPier does not collect, transmit, sell, or share data from the app. It has no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking, purchases, cloud service, third-party SDK, or network entitlement.

Decks, card text, study progress, and retained review ratings are saved locally in the app's sandbox. Scratch answers and active session queues are kept only in memory and are cleared when the card or session ends. The app retains up to 5,000 recent ratings and displays the latest 100. Undo/Redo history lasts only for the current app session.

Imports read only the files you choose in the system file panel and do not alter them. Exports write a new file to the location you choose. Portable decks omit study progress and history; full library backups include them. Restoring or resetting a library first preserves a local recovery copy. You decide whether to share exported files. A cloud-synced destination may be managed by its provider.

Help and privacy links open public GitHub pages in your browser. Visiting those pages is subject to GitHub's privacy policy. If you voluntarily submit a support issue, its contents may be public; avoid posting personal information or private study material. The app does not submit support reports automatically.

This policy covers RecallPier itself. Apple's App Store services and any optional operating-system diagnostics are governed by Apple's terms and privacy settings.

For questions, use [the support repository](https://github.com/valimikayilov/free-app-support/issues).
